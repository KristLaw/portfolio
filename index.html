The Filmmaker

About Me

I don’t just record moments — I run alongside them until they trust me enough to stay still.

I’m a filmmaker drawn to the in-between moments — the exhale before a race, the last frame of golden hour, the way a city looks when you’re the only one awake. My work spans abstract documentary, travel photography, sports filmmaking, and personal milestone storytelling. Each project is a journal entry with a lens.

Based everywhere I haven’t been yet. Currently chasing light and athletes in equal measure.

  • Camera: Sony FX3, A7IV
  • Edit: Premiere, DaVinci
  • Locations: 24+ countries
  • Races filmed: 60+ events
  • Films: Documentary, Sport
  • Photography: Travel, Portrait
