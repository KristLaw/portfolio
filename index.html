How I Work

The Method

01

Concept

↓

Every frame starts with a question, not a camera. I study the subject until I understand what they can't say out loud. Then I find the image that says it for them.

02

Craft

↓

Light, motion, rhythm. I shoot with Sony FX3 & A7IV, edit in DaVinci Resolve — every cut earns its place. No filler. No formula. Just the story the moment was already trying to tell.

03

Convert

↓

Great film changes something. Whether it's a viewer who finally feels seen, a brand that earns trust, or an athlete's defining moment preserved — the goal is always emotional impact that lasts.

The Filmmaker

About Me

I don’t just record moments — I run alongside them until they trust me enough to stay still. — P. Law

I’m a filmmaker drawn to the in-between moments — the exhale before a race, the last frame of golden hour, the way a city looks when you’re the only one awake. My work spans abstract documentary, travel photography, sports filmmaking, and personal milestone storytelling.

Based everywhere I haven’t been yet. Currently chasing light and athletes in equal measure.

  • Camera: Sony FX3, A7IV
  • Edit: Premiere, DaVinci
  • Locations: 24+ countries
  • Races filmed: 60+ events
  • Films: Documentary, Sport
  • Photography: Travel, Portrait
