The Filmmaker

About Me

I don’t just record moments — I run alongside them until they trust me enough to stay still.

I’m a filmmaker and biotechnology student drawn to the in-between moments — the exhale before a race, the last frame of golden hour, the way a city looks when you’re the only one awake. My scientific background sharpens how I observe; my camera captures what equations can’t.

My work spans abstract documentary, travel photography, sports filmmaking, and personal milestone storytelling. Currently based everywhere I haven’t been yet.

  • Degree: B.Sc Biotechnology
  • University: Chulalongkorn
  • Camera: Sony FX3, A7IV
  • Edit: Premiere, DaVinci
  • Locations: 24+ countries
  • Races filmed: 60+ events
